# TechMart Sales & Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

TechMart Sales & Business Performance Analysis is a data analytics project that examines sales data from a simulated technology retail business.

The project uses Python and Pandas to clean, analyze, and visualize sales data in order to identify product performance, regional performance, and monthly revenue trends.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- VS Code
- CSV

## Dataset

The dataset contains 23 sales records covering January to March 2026.

The dataset includes:

- Date
- Product
- Category
- Quantity
- Price
- Region

Products include laptops, smartphones, tablets, monitors, mice, keyboards, and headphones.

Regions include:

- Lagos
- Abuja
- Ibadan
- Port Harcourt

## Analysis Performed

The project analyzed:

- Total revenue
- Total units sold
- Average revenue per unit
- Revenue by product
- Units sold by product
- Revenue by category
- Revenue by region
- Average revenue per sale by region
- Monthly revenue
- Business insights from the analysis

## Key Findings

- Total revenue generated was ₦11,490,000.
- A total of 95 units were sold across 23 sales records.
- Smartphones generated the highest product revenue at ₦4,200,000.
- The Phones category generated the highest category revenue at ₦5,460,000.
- Lagos generated the highest total regional revenue at ₦5,235,000.
- March recorded the highest monthly revenue at ₦4,905,000.
- Mouse had the highest number of units sold with 23 units.
- Abuja had the highest average revenue per sale at ₦585,000.

## Visualizations

The project includes three visualizations:

1. Revenue by Product
2. Revenue by Region
3. Monthly Revenue
